# Fault-matrix integration gaps

Cost bands: **S** is at most two engineering days with existing local infrastructure; **M** is roughly three to five days or needs a new CI service; **L** is more than a week, needs external credentials/infrastructure, or materially lengthens CI.

| Case | Current coverage or skip | Real integration required | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
