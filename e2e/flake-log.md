# Fault-suite flake log

this matrix: a case is either in the suite and expected to pass, or it is not in
the suite. The 0.x matrix carried a rolling 100-run nightly history and a
promotion gate; both went with the nightly runner, and reinstating them means
reinstating a scheduled workflow first.

Record a flake here with enough detail that the next person can reproduce it:
the case, what failed, and what it turned out to be.
