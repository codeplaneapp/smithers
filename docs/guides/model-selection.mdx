Codex builds and reviews; Claude orchestrates and gates. Whenever usable
Codex authentication is available:

| Work | Model |
|---|---|
| The hardest implementation, final review, and the deepest reasoning | `gpt-5.6-sol` |
| **Substantial implementation, validation, and tool-heavy checking** | `gpt-5.6-terra` |
| Trivial, minimal-risk edits, cheap passes, and research gathering | `gpt-5.6-luna` |
| Orchestration and gating: scope, direction, progress, done-or-not | `claude-opus-4-8` |
| Planning, design freezes, and premium judgment calls | `claude-fable-5` |

Pin the exact tier IDs above instead of the floating `gpt-5.6` alias. Start
substantial implementation on Terra and escalate to Sol for ambiguity,
high-stakes decisions, novel architecture, final review, or repeated failure;
keep Luna on trivial, minimal-risk work only. Orchestration and gating
decisions (scope, choosing direction, judging progress, deciding whether
something is done) never go to GPT-5.6 Sol or Terra: they run on Claude, Opus
4.8 at medium reasoning effort by default, with Fable 5 for planning. See
[SOTA role defaults](/reference/sota-models#role-defaults).

When Codex is unavailable, Smithers falls back to detected Claude, Kimi,
Gemini, or other compatible adapters. Explicit provider-specific workflows
remain supported. The [SOTA model registry](/reference/sota-models) lists the
current defaults and badges and is refreshed by a daily research job.
