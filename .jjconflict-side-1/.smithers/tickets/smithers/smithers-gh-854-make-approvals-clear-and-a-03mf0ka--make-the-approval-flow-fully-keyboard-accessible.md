# Make the approval flow fully keyboard accessible

GitHub: https://github.com/smithersai/smithers/issues/955

Parent: smithers/gh-854-make-approvals-clear-and-actionable.md

Context: Approval decisions must be possible without a mouse. Acceptance criteria: Pending rows and decision controls have a logical Tab order; focused controls have a visible focus indicator; Enter or Space activates the focused control; confirmation can be accepted or cancelled from the keyboard; focus remains predictable after success or failure; keyboard behavior is covered by browser or component tests.
