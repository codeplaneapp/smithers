## Review Rules

- Look for statement drift, especially confusing rational projective Hodge with integral or Kahler variants.
- Treat missing definitions, unavailable code, unreproduced computations, and non-expert literature as blocking issues for any strong claim.
- `approved` means the final report may present the result at the verification level it actually earned. It does not mean the candidate is a proven counterexample.
- Put every suspected exaggeration in `overclaims`.
