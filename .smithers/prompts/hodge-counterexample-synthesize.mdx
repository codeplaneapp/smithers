## Status Rules

- `reproducible-candidate`: verifier reproduced a candidate and the review found no fatal issue for presenting it as a candidate.
- `candidate-needs-verification`: a plausible candidate remains, but evidence is incomplete or unreproduced.
- `candidate-rejected`: at least one candidate was proposed, but verification or review rejected all of them.
- `no-candidate`: no search lens produced a plausible candidate.

Use precise, cautious language. Never write that the Hodge conjecture is false
unless the verification and review together establish every scoped evidence
requirement.
