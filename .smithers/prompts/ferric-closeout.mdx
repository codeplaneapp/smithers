## Signed campaign closeout

The campaign has stopped. The reason: {props.reason}

Write the public closeout. This is the honest end of a project that promised to be falsifiable,
so it is written to the same standard as a success would have been.

Cover:

- What was attempted, and the scope it actually covered.
- The measured evidence that triggered the stop — the numbers, not the narrative.
- What was learned that is useful to someone attempting this again, including which
  assumptions turned out to be wrong.
- The run lineage: {props.lineage}
- The attested spend: {props.spend}

No spin, no "unfortunately", no framing the stop as a strategic pivot if it was a kill. A
project whose entire pitch was that someone else's tests decide whether it is lying does not
get to editorialize on the way out.

The durability demonstration stands on its own: one workflow ran this campaign end to end,
every gate was human-decided, and the evidence is inspectable. Say that plainly, once, and let
the reader weigh it.
