# Pitch Deck Template — modeled on the Airbnb (AirBed&Breakfast) 2008 seed deck

The Airbnb deck raised $600K on a $500K ask across 14 slides. Its power is its arc:
**pain → fix → why now → proof the market exists → how big → show the product → how we
make money → how we grow → who else is out there → why we win → who we are → external
validation → the ask.** Every slide makes exactly one claim, in one glance, with one
number where a number helps.

Rules the Airbnb deck follows (we follow them too):

- **One idea per slide.** If a slide needs a paragraph, it's two slides or zero.
- **Three bullets max**, bold the load-bearing word of each.
- **Every claim gets a number or a screenshot.** No adjectives doing the work of data.
- **Benefits, not features.** The Solution slide says what the user gets, not what the tech does.
- **Name your competition.** "No competitors" reads as "no market."
- **The ask is an opportunity**, framed as what the money buys, not what we need.

### Working notes

- Airbnb's deck is 14 slides because Why Now lives inside Problem/Validation; ours splits
  it out → 15. Cut back to 14 if Why Now merges into Problem during drafting.
- Order matters: never introduce the product before the pain, never show market size
  before proving the market is real, never show competition before the listener already
  wants us to win.
- Target: every slide readable in **under 10 seconds**. The deck should work with no
  presenter (it was an email deck).
